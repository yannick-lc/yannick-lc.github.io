### What is your blog about?
As I said, it's about machine learning.

To be more specific, I write about interesting use cases, underrated methods, and ways to easily adapt well-known tools to tackle seemingly not-so-easy problems.
In a nutshell, I write about what I wish I could have read 10 years ago, when I was starting out in the field.

### Who is it for?
My target audience is anyone with at least a rudimentary understanding of basic machine learning concepts like linear regression, supervised/unsupervised learning etc., and who is interested in learning more.
This should hopefully include some of my students, as the blog also serves as additional support material for [classes I teach](https://masterofscience-ia.com/).

### Do you write about your work for your clients?
Yes, this blog includes (or will soon include) practical case studies inspired by real work for real clients.

### But isn't it super sensitive?
Yes, but mostly no.

There is obviously no private client data used on this blog: all datasets are either publicly available or artificially generated to somehow resemble real data.
The same goes for the technical methods described here: they are typically fairly straightforward—although not necessarily obvious—ideas that have been simplified compared to the actual work done for my clients.

So you won't find any trade secrets here, sorry.

### What's up with this weird blog name?

Hey, watch your mouth! The domain name is my own company's, Manifold Technology. Turns out, the initials *almost* perfectly match a great blog name about machine learning, with just a teeny tiny bit of stretching to make it fit.

Other domain names were expensive, so that's the one I kept.
